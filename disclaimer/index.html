Medical disclaimer

PepCal is a personal logbook and calculator. It doesn’t diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and nothing in the app is medical advice.

It never suggests a dose

Measure and draw guides are arithmetic on the numbers you enter. Check every result against the instructions from your prescriber or the product label before you rely on it.

Learn is general information

Summaries in Learn describe published research in general terms. They aren’t recommendations, and many compounds covered are not approved for the uses people research them for.

Talk to a clinician

Check with a qualified clinician before you start, change or stop anything you take. If you think you’re having a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Sourcing and legality are yours

PepCal doesn’t sell, ship or endorse any compound or supplier. What’s legal where you live is up to you.

18 or older

PepCal is for adults. By using it you confirm you’re 18 or older.
