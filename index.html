What it does

  • Today. See your next dose with its syringe draw already worked out from your vial. Log it in one tap.
  • Stack. Schedule any compound: specific days, every N days, cycles, different doses on different days, or titration steps.
  • Supplies. Track vials, pens, capsules and tablets, with low-supply alerts before you run out.
  • Progress. Adherence, dose history, weigh-ins synced with Apple Health, and a body map for injection-site rotation.
  • Learn. A plain-language course and a compound library with safety information first and citations to published research.
  • Private by design. No account and no PepCal servers. Your data lives on your iPhone and syncs through your own iCloud. Face ID lock and CSV export are built in.

PepCal Pro starts with a 3-day free trial. Details are on the App Store listing.

PepCal is a personal tracking tool. It doesn't provide medical advice, diagnosis or dosing recommendations. The calculator converts the numbers you enter. Talk to a licensed clinician before starting, changing or stopping anything. Read the medical disclaimer.
